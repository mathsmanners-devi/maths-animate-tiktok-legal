Maths-Animate Devi

Privacy policy

Last updated November 7, 2026

Who operates this app

Maths Animate GH operates Maths-Animate Devi, a private tool used by the owner of the TikTok account @mathsanimate.gh. It is not a public-facing service and has no end-user registration.

Information the tool handles

  • TikTok authorization and account information: when the owner connects the app, TikTok processes the authorization request. With the granted permissions, the tool can receive OAuth tokens and request account identifiers and profile information, such as display name, open ID, follower count, and video count. Which fields TikTok returns depends on the app's approved permissions. Tokens are stored in local configuration on the operator's computer.
  • Video and lesson information: the tool reads locally stored lesson scripts and rendered videos to prepare TikTok-specific captions and a review queue. Caption generation is performed locally; this tool does not send lesson scripts to an AI-writing service.
  • Local operational records: queue state, publish history, publish identifiers, timestamps, file paths, and diagnostic logs may be stored locally to support review and prevent accidental duplicate publishing.

How information is used and shared

The tool uses account information to identify and check the connected TikTok account. When the operator explicitly starts an API operation, authorization data and the requested account information are sent to TikTok. If the operator explicitly publishes a video, the selected video and caption are sent to TikTok. The current direct-publish implementation requests public visibility and enables comments, Duet, and Stitch; the operator should review TikTok's publish screen and settings. The background review-queue scanner prepares items locally and does not publish them.

The tool does not operate a server or database that collects this information from other people. It does not sell personal information or use advertising or analytics trackers on this website. GitHub Pages hosts this policy site; GitHub may process technical request information to provide and secure hosting, subject to GitHub's privacy statement. TikTok processes information it receives under TikTok's privacy policy.

Storage and retention

OAuth tokens and operational files are stored on the operator's computer, not on a server operated by Maths Animate GH. They remain there until the operator removes them. The operator should protect access to that computer and its files. The app does not set a separate automatic deletion period. TikTok controls its own retention of information submitted to its services.

Your choices and deletion

The account owner can stop using the integration, remove local queue/history/configuration data, and revoke the app's access through TikTok's account or app settings. To ask about locally held information or make a privacy request, contact mathsmanners@gmail.com. Requests about information held by TikTok must be directed to TikTok.

Children

This is a private owner-operated tool, not a service for children or for collecting students' personal information. Lesson content should not include a student's personal information.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if the tool's data practices materially change. Contact mathsmanners@gmail.com with privacy questions.
